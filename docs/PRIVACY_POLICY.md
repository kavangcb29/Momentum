# Privacy Policy for Momentum

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026  

Welcome to **Momentum** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete ownership and control over your personal habit and productivity data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Momentum collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and web services.

---

## 1. Zero Cloud Data Collection (100% Local Storage)

- **Local Habit & Progress Data:** All your habits, streak histories, completion logs, notes, weight tracking, and protocol settings are saved **exclusively on your local device** using browser LocalStorage / IndexedDB.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need an account, email address, password, or profile to use Momentum.
- **No Central Servers:** We do not host central database servers that store or read your daily habits or personal reflections. If you uninstall the app or clear app data without exporting a backup, your local data is permanently cleared from your device.

---

## 2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Momentum displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob** to support ongoing development and maintenance. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information to deliver relevant ads:

### What Google AdMob May Collect:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, and hardware model.
- **Usage & Interaction Data:** Ad impressions, clicks, video views, and in-app ad events.
- **Diagnostic Information:** Crash logs, performance metrics, and network state for ad delivery reliability.

### How Google Uses This Information:
Google uses this data in accordance with Google’s Privacy & Terms:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:
You can manage or reset your advertising preferences at any time:
- **On Android Devices:** Open `Settings` > `Google` > `Ads` > Tap `Reset advertising ID` or toggle `Delete advertising ID`.
- **In-App Developer Bypass:** If authenticated via developer credentials, ad serving can be disabled entirely.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Backups

You retain 100% ownership of your habit and protocol records. You can export a full JSON backup of all your data at any time via the settings or developer modal, and save it anywhere you choose.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Momentum is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

---

## 5. Security

Because all personal productivity data remains on your physical device, security depends primarily on the protection of your device (device passcode, biometrics, and secure operating system updates).

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
