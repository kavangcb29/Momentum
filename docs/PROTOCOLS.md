## 🧠 Overview & Philosophy

The **Winter Arc** is not about temporary motivation or chaotic grind. It is a systematic, science-backed framework anchored by **Two Non-Negotiable Rules**:

1. **The Strict Execution Standard:** The exact target behavior on regular, high-energy days.
2. **The Minimum Resilient Floor:** The non-zero baseline fallback for days with extreme workload, illness, travel, or low energy. This completely destroys the "all-or-nothing" psychological trap and guarantees compound momentum.

---

## ⚡ The 5 Core Non-Negotiables

### 1. 🦩 11:00 PM Digital Sunset & 8-Hour Sleep Floor
- **Hex Color:** `#FF99C8`
- **Category:** `Circadian Shield & Sleep Recovery`
- **Point Weight:** `1 pt` (or custom point value)
- **Strict Rule:** Screens off strictly at 11:00 PM. Sleep >= 8.0 hours nightly and wake up before 8:00 AM without snooze.
- **Minimum Resilient Floor:** Bedtime alarm set for 11:15 PM even if total sleep duration is slightly shortened.
- **Scientific Rationale:** Protects REM and slow-wave sleep architecture required for muscle tissue repair, protein synthesis, and cognitive memory consolidation.

---

### 2. 🔥 4-Day Gym Split + Double Progression
- **Hex Color:** `#FF9233`
- **Category:** `Physical Hypertrophy & Strength`
- **Point Weight:** `1 pt` (or custom point value)
- **Schedule:** 4x/week (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday)
- **Strict Rule:** Train 4x/week on an Upper/Lower split with 4 sets per exercise. Hold weight static across all 4 sets until hitting 12 clean reps on EVERY set before raising load (+2.5–5 lbs).
  - **Mon:** Upper A (Bench Press, Incline DB Press, Overhead Press, Dips)
  - **Tue:** Lower A (Squats, RDLs, Leg Press, Calf Raises)
  - **Wed:** Active Recovery (10,000 steps + mobility stretching)
  - **Thu:** Upper B (Lat Pulldowns, Bent Rows, Lateral Raises, Bicep Curls)
  - **Fri:** Lower B (Bulgarian Split Squats, Hamstring Curls, Lunges, Abs)
  - **Sat & Sun:** Active Recovery / Rest
- **Minimum Resilient Floor:** 10-minute bodyweight circuit or active mobility flow.
- **Scientific Rationale:** Drives mechanical tension and myofibrillar hypertrophy while providing structured rest days to allow complete central nervous system and joint recovery.

---

### 3. 🟩 Clean Bulk & 80g Protein Floor
- **Hex Color:** `#A3FFB4`
- **Category:** `Nutrition & Fuel`
- **Point Weight:** `1 pt` (or custom point value)
- **Strict Rule:** Hit ~2,600 kcal/day clean surplus and >= 80g protein floor daily across clean whole-food meals.
- **Minimum Resilient Floor:** Hit 80g protein floor across Meals 1 & 2 even if total calories fluctuate.
- **Scientific Rationale:** Delivers necessary amino acids and intramuscular glycogen hydration to support muscle growth without causing digestive bloating during cognitive work.

---

### 4. 🌊 90-Minute Daily Study Sprint
- **Hex Color:** `#90E0EF`
- **Category:** `Technical Skill & Academics`
- **Point Weight:** `1 pt` (or custom point value)
- **Strict Rule:** Execute 90 minutes of deep work daily, split into three 30-minute blocks:
  - **30m SQL:** Practice window functions (`PARTITION BY`, `LAG`, `LEAD`, `DENSE_RANK`)
  - **30m BA Portfolio:** Process maps (Lucidchart), BRD user stories, or Power BI dashboards
  - **30m Coursework:** Computer Science assignments, lecture prep, or exam review
- **Minimum Resilient Floor:** 15 minutes reviewing SQL query syntax, project scope, or lecture slides.
- **Scientific Rationale:** Builds technical career capital through daily deliberate practice and strengthens neural pathways for complex analytical problem-solving.

---

### 5. 🎯 Daily Career Points System (3 Pts)
- **Hex Color:** `#FF70A6`
- **Category:** `Career Transition & Pipeline`
- **Point Weight:** `3 pts` (or custom point value)
- **Strict Rule:** Earn >= 3 Career Points every single day. Points reset to 0 at midnight. **NO BANKING / NO ROLL-OVERS.**
  - **1 Tailored Application** (Customized Resume & Cover Letter) = `2 Points`
  - **1 Networking / Referral Message** (LinkedIn outreach) = `1 Point`
  - **1 Quick-Apply Application** = `1 Point`
- **Minimum Resilient Floor:** Send 1 personalized networking/referral message on LinkedIn.
- **Scientific Rationale:** Ensures persistent daily action and pipeline volume during competitive recruitment cycles without burnout.
